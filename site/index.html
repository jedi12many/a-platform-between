You died, and woke at the Waystation, owing a fare. Make a traveler, then take the first train. It's the same game that runs on a Commodore 64: 40 columns, the same pictures, the same dice.

    How it works

    1. Make a traveler

      At the Waystation, name them, pick a race, a class and a skill, and buy or roll their stats. Press Issue the Passport, then Save to your travelers.

    2. Board the train

      Pick a mission on your traveler and press Board: no codes to type. When the trip ends, its Travel Stamp lands on them by itself. Have a Boarding Pass from elsewhere? Board a train and type it in.
