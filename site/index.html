You died, and woke at the Waystation, owing a fare. Make a traveler, then take the first train. The game is the same one that runs on a Commodore 64: 40 columns, with the same pictures and the same dice.

    1. Make a traveler at the Waystation

      Name them, then pick a race, a class and a skill, and buy or roll their stats. Press Issue the Passport, then Save to your travelers, with a note.

    2. Board the train

      Pick a mission on your traveler above and press Board: the Waystation issues their Boarding Pass and the train takes them straight on, with no codes to type. When the trip ends, its Travel Stamp lands on them by itself.

      Playing with a Boarding Pass or a Passport from elsewhere? Board here and type it in.

    3. Play

      • Press a number, or click a line, to choose.
      • Press S at a menu to save. Pick up a saved trip carries on from there.
      • In a fight: the arrow keys or the keypad move, Enter chooses, and a command's first letter does it (Move, Aim, Quick...).
      • Press ← Platform when the trip is over: your traveler is waiting there with what they earned, ready for the next mission.
    • Use a computer with a keyboard. The game reads keys, so a phone can make a traveler but can't board.
    • Saved trips stay in this browser. Your travelers follow your account when you're signed in.
    • After The Fare, Eighteen Minutes is on the same train menu: Departure 01, all five chapters.
