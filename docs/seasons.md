# Seasons

The game is sold in **seasons**. The base game is Season 1; each later season is a DLC.

## What a season is

Each season brings:

- **A new station** with its own **Stationmaster**, its own terms, and its own look.
- **New Platforms**, each a line of related Departures, or more Departures on existing
  lines, with a main story threaded through them and a finale. Lines are played in any
  order; see [lines.md](lines.md).
- **A new home biome**, with its own Tech Level and Magic Level that shape the station
  and its Departures.
- New races, classes, powers, items and Echoes, added to the shared registries.

| Season | Station | Stationmaster | Home TL / ML | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Waystation** | The Stationmaster (velvet coat, brass ledger) | 5 / 5 (a crossroads; everything half-fits) | In design |
| 2 | TBD | TBD | TBD | Ideas welcome |

Season 1 is where the hook lives: you died, you owe your fare, the Waystation is home.
Every new traveler starts with the **Arrivals** (*The Fare* and a few more short solo pieces
at the station), which come with the base game.
Later stations are the "A" in *A Platform Between*: there were always other stations.

## Carrying over

**Everything you accomplished carries over.** Your Passport (level, gear, Debt, Echoes,
Legend) moves to the new station and Translates into its terms, the same way gear
Translates between realms:

- **Gear** Translates to the new station's home levels.
- **Debt** carries over. Each station honors the others' ledgers, at its own exchange rate
  (a story beat, not a spreadsheet: one Stationmaster may forgive part of your debt and
  attach strings; another may add interest).
- **Echoes** carry over unchanged. A Season 2 Departure can pay off a Season 1 choice.

## Skipping a season

You can start any season without having played the earlier ones.

- A fresh character starts at the season's entry level with the season's starter kit.
- An existing character who skipped a season is filled in with **canon defaults**: every
  Echo in the registry has a default outcome, used whenever a Departure asks about a choice
  you never made. The Departure plays as if "someone" made the canon choice, and the hub
  gossip says so.
- Skipped Echoes stay **unset** on the Passport. If you later go back and play that season,
  your real choice replaces the default.

Inside a season, the same rules let you board a line's Departures out of order, and go
back for the earlier ones later: see *Boarding in any order* in [lines.md](lines.md).

## Replaying: Rewind

You can replay any Departure. In the fiction it's a **Rewind**: the Stationmaster sells you
a second ticket on a train that already left.

- A Rewind **replaces** the Echoes that Departure planted with your new choices, including
  any later transformations of those Echoes (if you rewind the dire wolf pup and leave it
  this time, `WOLF_PUP: SWORN` is gone too). Time travel makes this canon: the timeline
  shifted.
- First-clear rewards (XP, Debt reduction, unique loot) are paid **once per character**.
  Rewinds pay a reduced amount, so replaying is for the story, not for farming.
- A Rewind costs a small amount of Debt. The Stationmaster doesn't do favors.
- The hub notices. Regulars who remember the old timeline may comment.

## Money

- **Base game:** Season 1, its main-story Departures, the Waystation hub, the Sidings
  (side content, see [sidings.md](sidings.md)).
- **Season DLC:** new station, story, Departures, races and classes.
- **Standalone Departures** between seasons are possible (cheap one-offs), but the season is
  the main unit.
- Retro editions follow the same seasons, sold as disk/cartridge images and limited
  physical runs.
