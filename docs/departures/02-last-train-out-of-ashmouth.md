# Departure 02: The Last Train Out of Ashmouth

**Realm:** Ashmouth Wastes · **TL 6 / ML 1** · **Platform:** DOS · **Solo** · **Levels 2–5**

## The ticket

> "There is one train left in Ashmouth. Make sure it leaves on time, and that a particular
> passenger is on it."

## Hook

A post-apocalyptic rail line, the last running train, powered by a ghost-tech engine that
burns memories instead of coal. Bandits want the train; the town wants to board it; the
passenger the Stationmaster named doesn't want to go.

## Chapters

1. **Ashmouth.** A rail town on its last day. Meet the passenger: **Oren Vey**, a
   ten-year-old who builds radios.
2. **The rad-wolves.** Out in the scrub, a hunter's trap holds a dire wolf pup, glowing
   faintly. Saving it costs time you don't have.
3. **The Cutter gang.** Bandits led by **Jace Cutter**, nineteen and scared. You can break
   the gang, kill him, or let him go.
4. **The engine.** The engine needs memories to run. Whose? Yours is an option.
5. **Departure.** The train leaves. Who's on it decides Ashmouth's future, and Oren's.

## Notes

- The memory-fuel choice is a soft Dissonance lesson: burning your own memory costs a
  skill point until you return to the Waystation.
- Neither Jace outcome is "right." A spared Jace is ruthless later but honors the debt
  once; a killed Jace leaves a power vacuum someone worse fills.
