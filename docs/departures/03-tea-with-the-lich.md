# Departure 03: Tea With the Lich

**Realm:** The Lantern Courts · **TL 3 / ML 9** · **Platform:** Apple II · **Solo** · **Levels 3–6**

## The ticket

> "You've been invited to dinner. Someone at the table will die. Make sure it's the right
> someone."

## Hook

A dinner-party murder mystery where every guest is immortal, so murder takes real effort.
The host is **Lady Ysolde**, a lich who has hosted this dinner every century for a
thousand years. Text-heavy and conversation-driven; ideal for the Apple II.

ML9 means everything translates into magic: guns become wands, implants become
enchantments, Salvaged and Hollowborn characters are treated as honored golem-guests.

## Chapters

1. **Arrival.** The Moth-folk courtiers, the seating chart, the rules of etiquette (break
   them and Dissonance-style social penalties stack).
2. **The guests.** Seven immortals, each with a motive. Interview, snoop, bluff.
3. **The first death.** It wasn't the one you expected, and it didn't take.
4. **The ledger.** Ysolde has a brass ledger too. She was a traveler once, and she stayed.
5. **The toast.** Name the killer, or become one.
