# Departures

One-page treatments. Each lists its realm, levels, line, first gauge (the machine it
ships on first), chapters, and the Echoes it plants and listens for. Each of these three
opens a line; see [lines.md](../lines.md).

| # | Title | Line | Realm | TL/ML | First gauge | Mode | Level band |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | [Eighteen Minutes](01-eighteen-minutes.md) | Platform 1 | Station Kepler-Nine | 8 / 0 | C64 | Solo | 1–3 |
| 02 | [The Last Train Out of Ashmouth](02-last-train-out-of-ashmouth.md) | Platform 2 | Ashmouth Wastes | 6 / 1 | DOS | Solo | 2–5 |
| 03 | [Tea With the Lich](03-tea-with-the-lich.md) | Platform 3 | The Lantern Courts | 3 / 9 | Apple II | Solo | 3–6 |

Every Departure opens at the Waystation with a ticket from the Stationmaster and closes
there with the Debt payment and any Echo reactions at the bar.

Every Departure is written with **many roads to victory**: at least two routes to an
ending (fight, sneak, talk, solve, go around), tickets that hint at what to pack, and
roadblocks (enemies, puzzles, hazards, gatekeepers) some characters should turn away from. See
*Roadblocks and many roads* in [rules-v0.md](../rules-v0.md) and *Writing roadblocks and
many roads* in
[quest-script.md](../quest-script.md).
