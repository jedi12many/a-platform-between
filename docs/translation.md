# Translation

## Levels

Every realm has a **Tech Level (TL)** and a **Magic Level (ML)**, each 0–9.
Every item, spell, power and body has a native TL and ML.

| TL | Rough era | ML | Rough feel |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Stone | 0 | No magic at all |
| 2 | Bronze / iron | 2 | Folk charms, omens |
| 4 | Gunpowder | 4 | Hedge wizards, relics |
| 6 | Industrial / post-apocalyptic | 6 | Schools of magic |
| 8 | Spacefaring | 8 | Magic as infrastructure |
| 9 | Beyond | 9 | Reality is negotiable |

## What gets stored

The Passport stores what an item *is*, not what it looks like:

- **Archetype**: melee, ranged, armor, tool, focus, etc.
- **Tier**: its power, 1–9.
- **Damage/effect type**: kinetic, energy, fire, cold, mind, etc.
- **Origin**: its native TL/ML.

## Translating

When you enter a realm whose TL or ML is below an item's native level, the item
translates into the nearest form that realm supports. **Tier is conserved.** Only the look,
name and flavor change.

| Stored | Native (TL8) | TL2 realm | ML7, TL0 realm |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ranged · tier 4 · energy | Pulse Rifle | Heavy crossbow, burning bolts | Wand of lightning |
| Armor · tier 3 · kinetic | Ballistic weave | Lamellar coat | Warding tattoos |
| Tool · tier 2 · mind | Med-scanner | Herbalist's satchel | Seer's bones |

Bodies translate too: an android companion becomes a golem in a high-magic realm; an elf's
spells become psionics or nanites in a high-tech one.

When no form fits, the item becomes an **Unidentified Relic** for the duration of the
Departure. It still counts, still has its tier, and returns to normal at the Waystation.
Nothing is ever lost to Translation.

## Force It

You may refuse Translation for one item and carry it in its true form. It gets **+1 tier**
while forced, and generates **Dissonance**.

## Dissonance

A per-Departure counter, 0–9. Forcing an item adds 1 on arrival and 1 each time you use it
in a way the locals can see. Thresholds:

| Dissonance | Effect |
|---|---|
| 3 | Locals are wary; prices rise; some dialogue closes |
| 5 | Forced items can misfire (a failed roll may break them until you return) |
| 7 | The realm notices: hunters, inquisitors, or reality itself sends something |
| 9 | Backlash: you are pulled back to the Waystation, Departure failed, Debt added |

Dissonance is a choice, not a punishment. Some Departures are written so that forcing the
right item at the right moment is the best ending.

## Translation as compatibility

The same mechanic is the fallback when an engine can't support something. If the C64
engine has no vehicle rules, the hover-bike translates into a horse for that Departure.

## Platform ≠ tech level

The realm sets TL/ML; the platform sets the art style. A laser-noir Departure on the C64
and a stone-age one on the Amiga are both fine.
