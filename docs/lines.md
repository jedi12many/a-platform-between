# Arrivals and Platforms

How the story is laid out: a few short solo pieces when you arrive, then **Platforms**,
each a line of related Departures you ride in any order you like.

## Arrivals

Every new traveler starts with the **Arrivals**: a short run of solo Departures at the
Waystation itself, before any realm. They do two jobs:

- **Teach the game** a piece at a time: menus, a check, the concourse, the bar, a ticket.
- **Let your choices shape the character.** Stats are bought or rolled at the Waystation
  website, the way tabletop players expect (see [rules-v0.md](rules-v0.md)). The Arrivals
  settle the rest: who you are, not how strong you are.

| # | Arrival | What happens | What it can set |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Fare** (Departure 00, written) | You wake, remember dying, meet Fen and the Stationmaster, learn your Debt | First Echoes (what you told the Stationmaster, what you tried) |
| 2 | *The Ticket Window* (to write) | Your first small job, inside the station | A keepsake item |
| 3 | *Lost and Found* (to write) | Something of yours turns up that shouldn't exist | An Echo tied to how you died |

What the Arrivals hand out stays small and personal: Echoes, a keepsake, starting
relationships with the regulars. Nothing a fresh character needs to compete, so a player
who skips them isn't behind.

The Arrivals are played on any gauge (machine) like any Departure, and come back as a
receipt applied at the Waystation (see [boarding.md](boarding.md)). Characters are still
only made at the website.

**Proposed:** the Arrivals are required the first time you make a character on an account.
After that, a new character can take *"I've been here before"* at the ticket window: the
Arrivals are marked done with their canon defaults, and the player goes straight to the
platforms. They can still play them later, like any other Departure.

**Proposed, later:** move the extra tagged skill from the character creator into the
Arrivals, chosen by what you do rather than from a list. That needs receipts that can add a
tag (milestone E2), so creation stays as it is for now.

## Platforms

Each **Platform** is a line: a series of related Departures, usually in one realm, with a
shared cast, Echoes that run through it, and a finale. The departure board on the concourse
is the menu of lines.

| Platform | Line | Realm | Opens with |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | (tabletop: see [tabletop.md](tabletop.md)) | any | — |
| 1 | The Kepler-Nine line | Station Kepler-Nine, TL 8 / ML 0 | [Eighteen Minutes](departures/01-eighteen-minutes.md), levels 1–3 |
| 2 | The Ashmouth line | Ashmouth Wastes, TL 6 / ML 1 | [The Last Train Out of Ashmouth](departures/02-last-train-out-of-ashmouth.md), levels 2–5 |
| 3 | The Lantern Courts line | The Lantern Courts, TL 3 / ML 9 | [Tea With the Lich](departures/03-tea-with-the-lich.md), levels 3–6 |

Platform 0 is the exception, and the oldest: it isn't a line but the platform where any
line can be played by hand, at a table.

A Departure belongs to one Platform. Its level band still says who it's written for, but
it's never a gate (see below).

## Boarding in any order

**Order never gates.** Once a Platform is open to you (you own the season it belongs to),
you can board any Departure on it, first to last. The point is who can play together: a
party forms around a Departure, not around who has done the homework.

Skipping ahead uses the rules we already have:

- **Echoes:** every Departure must work with zero Echoes (see [echoes.md](echoes.md)). An
  Echo you never planted is *unset*, and the Departure uses its canon default. Regulars
  gossip that "someone" made that choice.
- **Level:** a level band is advice, not a lock. Boarding under the band is dangerous (the
  roadblocks will turn you back). Boarding over it is safe but slow: XP fades by 10% a level
  over the band, to nothing at 10 over ([rules-v0.md](rules-v0.md)), so old Departures
  stay worth playing for the story, not for farming.
- **The board shows the way:** the line's Departures are listed in story order, with the
  ones you've played stamped. Playing in order is the recommended road, never the only one.

### Going back

You can always board an earlier Departure on a line, after playing later ones. In the
fiction, it's an ordinary ticket on a train that left before the one you already rode; time
on the lines doesn't run in one direction.

- **It's a first trip, not a Rewind.** First-clear rewards are paid in full (faded only if
  you're over the band), and no Rewind fee is charged.
- **Your real choice replaces the default.** Skip *The Last Train Out of Ashmouth* and the
  later Ashmouth Departures play as if the wolf pup was left behind (`WOLF_PUP`'s canon
  default, `LEFT`). Go back, save the pup, and `WOLF_PUP: SAVED` goes on your Passport; the
  next Ashmouth Departure you board can pay it off. Anything a later Departure did *on top*
  of a default is undone, the same as a Rewind. The timeline shifted, and the regulars who
  remember the old one say so.
- **Replaying** something you've already played is still a Rewind (see
  [seasons.md](seasons.md)).

## Seasons and Platforms

A **season** is still what's sold (see [seasons.md](seasons.md)). Each season opens new
Platforms, or adds Departures to old ones, plus a main story that weaves through them and
ends in a finale. Owning a season opens its Platforms; the Arrivals come with the base game.

## Gauges: what we call machines in the story

The world needs a word for the machines the game runs on, now that "Platform" means a line.
On a railway, the **gauge** is the width of the track, and only trains built for it can run
on it. So the machines are gauges:

- *"The Kepler-Nine line runs on every gauge."*
- The C64 is **narrow gauge**: smaller trains, the same stations.
- The website is the station itself, and tabletop is Platform 0.

In engineering docs, "platform" can still mean a target machine
([platforms.md](platforms.md)); in anything a player reads, use *gauge*.

## Open questions

- Should a line's finale need anything (a minimum level, or most of the line played)? The
  proposal is no: the finale's roadblocks and level band do that job.
- How many Departures make a line? Proposal: 3–5 in a season, more added in later seasons.
- Can a party board a Departure some members have played and others haven't? Proposal: yes.
  Members who have played it take it as a Rewind (reduced rewards, no fee for joining a
  friend), everyone else as a first trip.
