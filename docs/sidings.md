# Sidings: content outside the story

On a railway, a siding is a short side track off the main line. In *A Platform Between*,
**Sidings** are everything that isn't a story Departure: places to wander, grind, gamble
and tinker. Some players will spend most of their time here, and that's fine.

## Rules for all Sidings

1. **Your character benefits.** XP, materials, items, coin, perks, cosmetic marks.
2. **Sidings never replace the story.** They pay little or no Debt. Only Departures move
   you meaningfully toward going home.
3. **They respect level bands.** Rewards scale down when you outlevel a Siding, and
   Departures soft-scale anyway, so grinding helps without breaking the story.
4. **Same rules, same Passport.** A Siding is played with the same rules core and writes
   to the same Passport.
5. **They can plant small Echoes**, of the Mark kind (reputations and titles), never story
   choices.

## Kinds

### The Deep Yards: procedural dungeons

Beneath the Waystation are rail yards that go down further than they should: tracks to
dead and half-remembered realms. Each descent is procedurally generated from a seed:

- Every floor rolls a realm (TL/ML), so Translation is in play constantly.
- Deterministic: the same seed builds the same dungeon on every platform. Seeds can be
  shared ("try yard 7Q4K, floor 9 is nasty").
- A good fit for the retro engines; procedural content costs almost no disk space.

### Mini-games at the station

Each mini-game feeds a part of your character:

| Mini-game | Where | Your character gains |
|---|---|---|
| **Fare** (a card game played with old tickets) | The bar | Coin, rumors, the occasional rare ticket |
| **Handcar racing** | The freight lines | Grace training, racing titles |
| **Salvage sorting** (a puzzle) | Lost-and-found | Crafting materials, odd items |
| **Tinkering bench** (combine and re-Translate gear) | Traders' row | Custom items, Tinker perks |
| **Fishing the Between** | The edge of the platforms | Strange catches, Fate training, a very patient Stationmaster joke |

Ideas, not commitments. Mini-games are cheap to test and cheap to cut.

### Daily and weekly trains (online)

Hub-only: a shared seed for the Deep Yards each day, with leaderboards. Later, party yards.

## Platform notes

The Deep Yards and one or two mini-games should ship on every retro platform. They make a
small retro release feel much bigger. Some mini-games may be modern-only.
