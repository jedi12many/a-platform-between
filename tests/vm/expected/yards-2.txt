=== The Deep Yards
[picture 0 yards]
At the far end of the freight lines, past the last lamp, a cage lift hangs over a shaft that goes down further than the Waystation does. A brass plate on the gate says YARD 1944. Someone has scratched a number under it: 10.
The operator, a man made mostly of rust and patience, doesn't look up. "Ten floors," he says. "Every one a different place. Come back up when you like. If you can."
  1) Step into the cage
  2) Not today

A dust world. Sand drifts over the sleepers, and the wind smells of hot iron.

  1) A dark siding: something nests there
  2) Take the stairs down
  3) Ride the cage back up
