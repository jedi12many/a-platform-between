At the far end of the freight lines, past the last lamp, a cage lift hangs over a shaft that goes down further than the Waystation does. A brass plate on the gate says YARD 638. Someone has scratched a number under it: 10.
The operator, a man made mostly of rust and patience, doesn't look up. "Ten floors," he says. "Every one a different place. Come back up when you like. If you can."
