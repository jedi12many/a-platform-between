As you step aboard, the world takes your measure. Kepler-Nine is a place of machines and no magic at all, and you change to fit it.

=== Again
You stop in the middle of the ring and let the station run past you.
Eighteen minutes. A dead freighter you could set a watch by. A man who will never remember your name, however many times you tell him. A safe at the top of the spine.
And one voice, in your ear, that remembers everything.
The screens say 10 minutes. For the first time since you stepped off the train, it doesn't feel like very long, and it doesn't feel like very short. It feels like a door.
You go to find the crew.
